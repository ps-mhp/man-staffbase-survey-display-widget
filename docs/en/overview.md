# Survey Widget

The Survey Widget embeds an existing Staffbase survey installation
at the location where the widget is placed on a page. The
survey itself continues to be managed via the Staffbase Surveys app
(questions, timeframe, analysis)—this widget simply displays the
already-created survey in an additional location, such as on a
home page or team page, rather than just in the Surveys section.

To ensure the survey integrates correctly into the page, the widget
automatically adopts contextual information such as language and text direction from the
surrounding page—no additional settings are required.
